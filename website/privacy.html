Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

What we collect

Your name, phone number (used only to sign in and never shown to other users), profile photo, and, if you list as a worker, your category, city, experience, description, contact number and work photos. Chat messages between users are stored so conversations can be delivered.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We receive only the payment status, order ID and payment ID. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use it

To run the app, show worker listings, enable chat, prevent spam and provide support. We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

Worker listing details are visible to other registered users. We share data with service providers (hosting by Supabase, payments by Razorpay) only as needed, or when required by law.

Your choices

You can edit your profile in the app, or email us at sahayi2026@gmail.com to request deletion of your account and data.
