Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

The ₹149 contact unlock and the ₹149 worker-profile fee are one-time digital charges and are non-refundable once your account has been unlocked, except in the cases below.

When we refund

  • You were charged more than once for the same registration.
  • Money was deducted but the feature was not unlocked.

How to request

Email sahayi2026@gmail.com with your registered email and Google Play order ID within 7 days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Google Play's own refund rules also apply.

Cancellation

There is no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. You can stop using the app at any time and request account deletion by email.
