Delete your Sahayi account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and data for the Sahayi app (Android package com.sahayiapp.in, developer: Jestin George).

Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Sahayi app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete Account under Settings.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to sahayi2026@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject "Delete my Sahayi account" and include your name as shown in the app.
  3. We reply to confirm it is you, then delete the account. This is done within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email and login).
  • Your worker listing: service, city, experience, description, phone number, availability and contact settings.
  • Your profile photo and work photos.
  • Your chat messages and conversations.
  • The ratings and reviews you wrote.

What we may keep

  • Records of purchases made through Google Play (order ID, product, date), kept only as long as tax and accounting laws require. They are no longer linked to a usable account.
  • Copies in routine backups, which are overwritten on the normal backup cycle.

Good to know

  • Deleting your account cannot be undone. Purchases you made (contact unlock, worker-profile listing) are tied to the account and are not restored after deletion. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.
  • If you only want to hide yourself, you can instead set your status to Booked, set your number to "Only me" and turn "Get messages" off in the app.
