Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Sahayi ("we", "us") is an app that connects people with local home-service workers such as electricians, plumbers, carpenters and cleaners. It is operated by Jestin George, India. This policy explains what information the Sahayi app (Android package com.sahayiapp.in) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. By using Sahayi you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot read them.

Worker profile (only if you list yourself as a worker). Your name, service category (or the custom service name you type under "Other"), city and country, years of experience, a short description, profile photo, up to three work photos, your phone number (optional), your Available/Booked status and your contact preferences.

Messages. Chat messages you send and receive in the app, with time stamps and read status, so conversations can be delivered and shown to you.

Purchase information. When you buy the contact unlock or the worker-profile listing through Google Play, Google tells us the purchase status, order ID, product and purchase token. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

Photos you choose. Pictures you pick from your gallery for your profile or work gallery. Sahayi only accesses the photos you select.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your device location, contacts, call logs or SMS. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not show ads.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show worker listings in search results.
  • To let customers contact workers by chat or phone, as allowed by the worker's contact settings.
  • To verify purchases and unlock the features you bought.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and misuse, and to give you support.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who can see what

  • Worker listings (name, service, city, experience, description, photos, rating, availability) are visible to other signed-in Sahayi users.
  • Your phone number is never shown in the listing. If you set "Who can see my number" to Public, a signed-in user who has paid the contact unlock can call you. If you set it to Only me, nobody else can get your number.
  • Messages are visible only to the two people in the conversation. A worker can turn "Get messages" off so that nobody can start a new chat with them.
  • Your email address is not shown to other users.

4. Sharing with service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the app, and only as needed:

  • Supabase – hosting of our database, user authentication, file storage (photos) and realtime chat.
  • Google Play – processing of in-app purchases.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users. We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to the database is restricted with row-level security so users can read and change only what they are allowed to. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time, see how to delete your account. After a deletion request we remove your profile, worker listing, photos and chat messages within 30 days. We may keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit your name, listing, photos, availability and contact settings in the app. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data by emailing sahayi2026@gmail.com. You can also revoke photo access in your phone's settings.

8. Children

Sahayi is not meant for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be notified in the app or on this page.
